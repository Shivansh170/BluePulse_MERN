🌊 BluePulse – Real-Time Water Body Monitoring & Survey Platform

Live Website: https://blue-pulse-mern.vercel.app/

BluePulse is a full-stack MERN application designed to monitor, track, and analyze water bodies across India with the help of surveyor input, admin management, and real-time dashboards.
Built to support smart environmental monitoring, BluePulse enables users to explore water bodies, view survey data, and predict water quality.

🌐 User Workflow (Normal User)

A normal user visiting BluePulse follows this flow:

1. Open the landing page


2. Read about what BluePulse does


3. Use the search bar to find a water body


4. View all survey results, statistics, and details


5. Predict the water quality via the
“Predict Water Quality” button


6. Explore further details or visit other water bodies



This ensures maximum accessibility even for users without an account.
